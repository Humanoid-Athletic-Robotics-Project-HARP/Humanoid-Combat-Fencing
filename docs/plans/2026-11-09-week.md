## BeyondMimic: reading questions

Be able to answer these by the end of Monday:

1. What goes into the policy, and what comes out? What does it observe about the reference motion?
2. How is the tracking reward set up? What is an "anchor" body and why use one?
3. How does it decide which parts of a motion to train on more often (adaptive sampling)?
4. What does it randomize in simulation, and why does that matter for the real robot?
5. Why can one fixed set of training settings work for every motion, and what kind of motions does it fail on?
6. What does the guided diffusion part add on top of motion tracking? (This is what Goals 2–4 will need later; skim it this week.)
7. How long does training one motion take, and on what GPU?
8. What reference motion format does `whole_body_tracking` expect, and does GMR's output match it?

Question 8 matters most for this week. **Answered 2026-11-08: the formats match**, and GMR's `batch_gmr_pkl_to_csv.py` writes the expected CSV. See [`../beyondmimic_notes.md`](../beyondmimic_notes.md#8-reference-motion-format-and-does-gmrs-output-match-).

## Finding videos: what makes a good clip

Log links only, never download or commit the videos. Each row goes in [`data/video_log.md`](../../data/video_log.md).

**Good clips:**
- Saber specifically (footwork and arm motion differ from foil and épée)
- One fencer, or one fencer clearly separated from others
- Camera from the side, steady, with the whole body including the feet in frame
- Good lighting, and the fencer stands out from the background
- Drills, lessons and footwork tutorials are better than bouts: slower, more repetitive, no overlap between fencers

**Rough mix for ~50:**

| Motion | Clips |
|---|---|
| En garde (holding still) | 5–10 |
| Advance / retreat | 15 |
| Lunge (with recovery) | 15 |
| Advance-lunge | 5–10 |
| Parries / cuts (for later, with the saber) | 5–10 |

**Search ideas:** "saber footwork drill", "sabre lunge tutorial", "fencing advance retreat drill", federation and club coaching channels, slow-motion lesson footage.
